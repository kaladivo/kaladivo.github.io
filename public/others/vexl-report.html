Business impact

The work improves product confidence in four concrete ways

More polished product perception

The redesigned app should feel more coherent and current, which matters for trust in a product built around private peer-to-peer exchange.

Better marketplace conversion paths

Users can browse, filter, map, inspect, create, and act on offers with fewer dead ends and clearer states.

Safer trading behavior

The chat checklist, calculator, identity steps, and clearer end states help users make decisions with more structure.

Stronger launch and operations capability

Version-aware notifications, backoffice controls, localization workflows, and bug fixes reduce launch risk for future product changes.

Bottom line: the last three months look like a coordinated push to modernize the Vexl app, improve marketplace trust and liquidity paths, and prepare the product for broader, cleaner communication with users.
