A1

HOW I WORK

My goal is to multiply other team members

In my view, the role of engineers is not to write code anymore, but to "multiply" other team members (or the community in case of open source). I enjoy setting up the repo, code and tooling around agentic development. If this is done correctly, anyone with access to the source code can make good and working contributions. In the age of AI this is what I believe we should strive towards.

Tasks that are well defined and articulated are for clankers. Humans are here to understand the broader context and define what needs to be done.

I love doing work where I am not just implementing well defined tickets. I don't view myself as a programmer (although I have always spent most of my time around code). I view myself more like a problem solver specialized in technical solutions. I strive to understand the product and needs of users and others in the team to find solutions that best move us towards fulfilling them.
